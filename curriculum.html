Three notes on the plan behind this

1. The plan has no static testing task

Reviews and static analysis are a whole chapter of the ISTQB Foundation syllabus, and in practice the cheapest defect removal available — a defect caught in a sentence costs nothing to fix. Lesson 3 was written anyway. It is the only lesson here with no task ID.

2. QA-004 asks for gray-box testing

ISTQB v4.0 does not define gray-box as a test type — it recognises black-box and white-box only. Gray-box is real industry vocabulary (driving the UI while using knowledge of the API or database underneath) but it comes from practice, not the syllabus. Lesson 2 currently teaches the two the syllabus defines, which is why it is marked partial. The gray-box section is worth adding with that distinction made explicit, because an interviewer may well use the term.

3. Three lessons are waiting on sources

Lesson 4 (test oracles) and lesson 10 (test management tools) have no trustworthy source identified yet, and lesson 9 (bug reports) needs a route to ISTQB §5.5 that actually includes the defect-report contents list. Those get written after the reading, not from memory — a course that invents its facts is worse than no course.
