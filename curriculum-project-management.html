Two problems worth fixing early

Most tasks point at one roadmap link. Around forty of the sixty-three cite the same roadmap page. A roadmap is a map, not a textbook — it names topics without teaching them and gives no worked examples. Those lessons need a real source found before they can be written.

PMBOK is paywalled. It is cited for eight tasks and is not freely readable. Either budget for access, or find an open equivalent for charter, WBS, earned value and reserves — and decide that before week 2, when the charter lesson lands.
